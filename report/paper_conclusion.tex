\section{Conclusion}
This study measures learned decisions through complete Kira reduction time. The local seed and equation comparisons support their use within the tested domains, while the row model adds no reliable saving. The retained method therefore keeps native elimination rows and places learned decisions before work that they can avoid.

The shared structural model extends the comparison beyond family-specific choices. It learns from 74 cost-labelled requests, uses 51 separate validation requests, and is tested on a new 110-request grid. The 43 matched nontrivial reductions give time ratios 0.900 against native choices and 0.951 against the common fixed setting. The 15 matched requests from excluded physical groups give ratios 0.939 and 0.885, respectively. The timing intervals and coverage table determine the scope of these measurements.

Compact preparation without ML compared with expanded discovery gives ratio 0.305 on 13 matched requests, with repeat interval [0.290,0.329]. The neural model within compact preparation gives ratio 0.982 on 22 matched requests, with repeat interval [0.959,1.021]. The cost-and-risk model within compact preparation gives ratio 1.635 on 22 matched requests, with repeat interval [1.604,1.700]. The cost-and-risk model increases time in this broad comparison. The neural preparation result also remains consistent with equal time. We keep the failure model and its records for analysis, while the shared neural model remains the validated supplied-basis default. The cache supplies only previously checked master indices, and every new target is reduced and checked at its own parameters. The preparation change and the learned choice therefore receive separate controls.

The default keeps native preparation and uses the shared neural model when a master basis is supplied. The exact scalar formula takes priority when its physical assumptions hold.

The focused follow-up adds basis refresh when a stored basis cannot reduce a new batch. Its complete outcomes and matched model comparisons appear in Section~\ref{sec:preparation}.

The remaining limitation is native coverage. Defining and scoring an input through twelve loops does not show that its full IBP reduction completes. Unsupported coordinates, failed reference calculations, general symbolic kinematics, and more varied graph structures require further verified comparisons. Exact scalar formulas and reuse can supply separate savings under their assumptions, and the notes retain those results without attributing them to ML.
