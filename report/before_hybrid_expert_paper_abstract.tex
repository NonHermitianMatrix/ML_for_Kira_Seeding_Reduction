\begin{abstract}
Integration-by-parts reduction expresses Feynman integrals in a master basis~\cite{KiraOriginal}. We train a cost-sensitive selector to choose among nine Kira seed settings from the integral structure and required master basis. Training minimizes measured application time, including unsuccessful attempts and recovery. Exact formulas and independent-loop factorization precede prediction, while native settings remain available. The reserved comparison contains 65 requests, of which 64 complete three exact checks. On matched completions, the updated application uses 8.5\% less time than the previous application. Validation retains a fixed setting for deployment. The timers include model use, equation generation, algebra, exact checks, and recovery; master-basis preparation and model training precede these measurements. We keep the dimension symbolic and use rational values for the other kinematic variables. The tests cover coupled native families through three loops and specified formulas and independent products through thirteen loops. General high-loop scattering and globally minimum reduction paths remain unverified.
\end{abstract}
