\begin{abstract}
We study how exact identities and learned choices reduce the time needed to decompose loop integrals into master integrals with Kira. The method applies proved identities to supported integral structures and uses a model trained on complete reduction costs for the remaining choices. Its exact domains include connected one-scale bubble trees through thirteen loops. We extend the method to polynomial numerators in one-loop sectors and independent products of these sectors. Angular tensor averages and signed-index IBP give the reduction, while their dimension poles permit exact arithmetic with factored linear denominators. On 20 matched one-loop tensor requests, complete time changes from 53.518 to 6.343 seconds relative to the preceding application. The final extension completes 44 of 44 reserved requests in every repetition, and total time across this collection changes from 227.011 to 62.225 seconds. Separate controls measure the retained native seed model. We distinguish exact savings from learned savings, include recovery costs, and retain unsuccessful requests in the coverage counts. Learned row selection remains omitted. The results cover the defined families and kinematics, with no claim of exhaustive high-loop scattering coverage.
\end{abstract}
