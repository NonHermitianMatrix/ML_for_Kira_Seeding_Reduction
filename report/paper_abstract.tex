\begin{abstract}
Integration-by-parts reduction expresses Feynman integrals in a master basis~\cite{KiraOriginal}. We combine exact formulas, independent-loop factorization, basis-compatible native settings, and a trained sparse-seed expert. An outer model learns complete route costs, including prediction, equation generation, algebra, exact checks, and recovery. Separate validation compares the resulting method with the selected ML-free control before enabling learned decisions. The hybrid test contains 34 requests, of which 34 pass three exact checks. For 24 matched requests, the integrated method takes 136.956 seconds and the validation-selected ML-free control takes 143.079 seconds. This gives 4.3\% less time, with ratio 0.957 and repetition interval $[0.925,0.998]$. The comparison with the selected ML-free control resolves an added saving from learned decisions beyond the measured repetition variation. On the 8 requests with index vectors absent from outer-model training, the method takes 9.9\% more time than the preceding application. A separate 65-request baseline test covers specified analytic families and independent products through thirteen loops, while coupled native calculations reach three loops. The sparse-expert comparison uses a nonplanar two-loop family with numerator ranks two through four. We keep the dimension symbolic and use rational values for other invariants. These timings assume a prepared master basis; training and independent reference preparation remain separate costs. General high-loop scattering and globally minimum reduction paths remain unverified.
\end{abstract}
