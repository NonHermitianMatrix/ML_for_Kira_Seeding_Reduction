\begin{abstract}
We study how exact integral identities and machine learning reduce the time needed to express loop integrals in terms of master integrals with Kira. The application first integrates supported subgraphs analytically, then uses trained choices to select the remaining native reduction work. We derive exact rational coefficients for connected massless bubble trees with either a one-scale triangle or a two-point root. Recursive bubble integration retains dimension-dependent powers, and cancellation of Gamma factors produces finite rational products. The new two-point comparison completes all 34 defined structures through thirteen loops. On its 3 matched completed requests, time falls from 11.185 to 0.433 seconds. A separate triangle comparison reduces large-batch time from 14.305 to 6.731 seconds by cancelling factors before expansion. These gains come from exact algebra. The retained ML policy has time ratio 0.937 against fixed choices on sixteen fresh native box requests. Its request interval includes equal time. Earlier controlled tests establish separate seed and equation-selection gains. Learned row selection remains omitted. All timings include the stated application costs, and failed controls remain in the coverage results. The analytic domains have explicit mass, kinematic, and numerator restrictions; general high-loop scattering remains outside the demonstrated coverage.
\end{abstract}
